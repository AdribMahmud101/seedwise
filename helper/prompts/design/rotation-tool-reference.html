How NASA Earth Observations & Local Trial Data Are Combined

Dataset / Source Resolution Role in 3D Field & Rotation Tool Key Limitation / Uncertainty
NASA POWER (MERRA-2 /GWET) ↗ 0.5° × 0.625° · Daily (2001–26) Daily rain, Tmax/Tmin, FAO-56 ET₀, surface (`GWETTOP`) & root-zone (`GWETROOT`) wetness Regional grid cell; cannot resolve sub-parcel moisture differences
Sentinel-2 L2A (Copernicus) ↗ 10 m · 307 dates (2021–26) True-colour basemaps, seasonal NDVI composites, bare-field calendar (May–Jun gap) Monsoon cloud (~92% cloudy in July) blocks optical views
Sentinel-1 SAR RTC ↗ 10 m · 538 scenes (3 orbits) Cloud-penetrating VH/VV backscatter for rice transplanting & monsoon open-water ponding Requires 3×3 speckle filter and all 3 orbits (4-day revisit)
NASA JPL NASADEM ↗ 30 m (1 arc-sec) 3D parcel elevation mesh, slope %, flood exposure percentile (21st vs 73rd percentile) Cannot see 15 cm earthen bunds (aal) or field culverts
Sarker et al. (2026) RARS Trial ↗ Burirhat Station · 2021–23 Measured soil baseline (pH 5.61, OM 1.83%) & 4 cropping patterns (CP4, CP5, CP11, CP12) 2 trial seasons at 1 station; market prices vary
BARC Fertilizer Guide 2024 ↗ AEZ-3 (Tista Floodplain) Crop-specific elemental N, P, K doses and regional pH/micronutrient (Zn, B, S) status Regional guideline; verify with parcel soil test
