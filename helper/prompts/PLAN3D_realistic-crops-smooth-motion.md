1. **Crop-specific procedural models** (instanced, performant — thousands of plants on phones): Rice hills (clumps of thin arching leaf blades, green→golden panicles by stage), Potato (bushy compound-leaf plants on ridges/furrows), Mustard (upright stems, broad lower leaves, yellow flower clusters), Mungbean/lentil (low trifoliate bushy plants), Wheat/maize if present in the planner's crop list. Natural variation (height, rotation, colour jitter), soft shadows, ground with soil texture, ridges for potato, bunds/paths where reserved area is excluded.
2. **Smooth transitions** (ease, ~400–700 ms): spacing/usable-area change → plants glide to new grid positions (instanced matrix tween), added plants grow in from 0, removed ones shrink out; crop change → old crop shrinks/fades, new crop grows in with a row-wise stagger; camera eases to frame the field after size changes; brief highlight of changed rows. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
3. **Camera & view**: field fills the preview (~85 % width) with a good default angle; 3D/Top toggle animates; subtle idle sway (small, like the studio); zoom/rotate by drag/pinch. A close-up "inspect a plant" zoom on click is a bonus.
4. **Performance**: ≥45 fps on a mid phone (InstancedMesh, LOD: far plants simpler), cap instance count with honest note if the real count exceeds it ("showing every 4th plant").
