NASA data 11

#Dataset nameLinkHow you used it
1NASA POWER (MERRA-2 based, daily + monthly API)https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/Temperature, humidity, solar radiation, wind and rain for each field, 1981–2025: My field weather cards, Past seasons charts, and the training and backtest data for our Chronos-Bolt forecaster.
2NASA POWER soil wetness (GWETTOP / GWETROOT)https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/daily/Surface and root-zone soil wetness history; the "soil drier than normal" signal in Today's analytics and a forecast target (about 60% lower error than the seasonal average).
3FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration from NASA POWERhttps://power.larc.nasa.gov/ETo × crop coefficient = crop water demand, used in the water-use card, rotation water scores and plan numbers.
4NASA GPM IMERG (0.1° precipitation)https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/imergDaily and monthly rainfall 2021–2025 per field (via the POWER API); Past seasons year-by-year rainfall and a cross-check against POWER rain.
5NASA SMAP L4 Soil Moisture (SPL4SMGP, 9 km)https://nsidc.org/data/spl4smgp/versions/8Daily surface (0–5 cm) and root-zone (0–100 cm) moisture 2021–2025 with percentiles, read from POWER's public SMAP store; field soil-moisture cards and the plan pipeline "water" step.
6NASA HLS v2.0 (Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2, 30 m)https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/hls2024–2025 monsoon scenes over each field fed into our fine-tuned Prithvi flood model; cloudy scenes skipped with a reason (23 of 201 clear). Drives the flood signal in Today's analytics and the water check.
7NASA–IBM Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M-TL foundation modelhttps://huggingface.co/ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-2.0-300M-TLFine-tuned by us on Sen1Floods11 for land/water segmentation (water IoU 82.0, mIoU 89.7), served on Modal and run daily on HLS scenes for each field.
8NASA JPL GRACE / GRACE-FO Mascons (RL06.3 v04)https://podaac.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/TELLUS_GRAC-GRFO_MASCON_CRI_GRID_RL06.3_V4Groundwater storage anomaly for the Rangpur region; the plan pipeline's water-resilience step and 3D studio groundwater context.
9NASADEM (30 m elevation, NASA JPL)https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/lpcloud-nasadem-hgt-001Low-ground map layer showing where water collects, plus the 3D terrain of the field and landscape (accessed via Microsoft Planetary Computer).
10Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level 2 (NASA/USGS)https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-surface-reflectanceSurface reflectance and QA bands (cloud masking) for field imagery and greenness; scene IDs and dates kept with each record.
11NASA Earthdata / CMR searchhttps://cmr.earthdata.nasa.gov/search/Finding and checking GRACE-FO and HLS granules before download.
