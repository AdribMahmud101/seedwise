Sentinel-1 processing: `scripts/fetch-s1-water.py` reads the current pilot polygons through the app's `basePolygon` function. Each site uses one orbit and direction with all twelve months available, minimizing the total acquisition-time distance from each month's 15th at 00:00 UTC. Each monthly record selects the nearest acquisition within that month, with item ID breaking ties. Selection does not depend on water percentages.

VV is sampled at its native 10 m spacing on a common EPSG:32645 grid covering the field bounding box plus at least 200 m. Nearest-neighbour sampling preserves observed values. A measured one-pixel halo supports a 3×3 median filter in linear gamma0. Conversion uses `10*log10(gamma0)`, following [Microsoft's RTC example](https://github.com/microsoft/PlanetaryComputerExamples/blob/main/datasets/sentinel-1-rtc/sentinel-1-rtc-example.ipynb). Values are rounded to four decimal places before classification. Water means VV strictly below −18 dB. Otsu uses 256 bins over the entire buffered context and reports a separate threshold and field percentage. Flooded percentages count only pixel centers inside the traced polygon; the 200 m context is not the field denominator. Missing, nonpositive or invalid samples abort the fetch.

These are radar-derived open-water classifications, not ground measurements or flood depths. Effective SAR resolution is approximately 20×22 m despite 10 m sampling. Small fields, crop cover and the median filter limit water detection. Otsu can partition crops, roads or buildings even where no water is present. Reproduce with the Python environment and command in the script's docstring; no JavaScript dependencies or lockfiles need to be changed to fetch the data.

DATAS1 acceptance discrepancy: the selected acquisitions yield 0% fixed-threshold water inside all three traced fields in every month. The nonbinary seasonal acceptance checks remain strict and fail; the fetch script saves the observed pack and then exits with that failure. An independent diagnostic also read Mithapukur's other complete annual track: the lowest filtered field VV across both eligible tracks was −15.9459 dB, above the required −18 dB threshold. Changing orbit cannot satisfy that site's nonbinary check under this method. The buffered context contains some darker pixels, but counting those as field flooding would change the requested denominator. Neither Otsu percentages nor nearby water replace the fixed-threshold field result.
