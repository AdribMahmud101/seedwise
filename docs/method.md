# Methods and limitations · 3 November 2026

**Spatial support.** Initial field outlines and planning profiles are illustrative. Area uses a local equirectangular projection and shoelace formula. Redrawing an outline changes planting area. Climate estimates and optical samples retain their original grid or window. Nearby fields may share the same regional value.

**Weather and wetness.** POWER supplies temperature, corrected precipitation, radiation, humidity, pressure, wind and modeled wetness on a ~0.5° × 0.625° (MERRA-2) grid. IMERG rainfall is requested independently through POWER. The POWER meteorology anchors are 89.375° E / 25.5° N and 89.375° E / 26.0° N. IMERG requests use the three prepared centres. Missing values remain null. GWETTOP and GWETROOT are dimensionless indices. A logger can record a separate local probe reading with its depth and volumetric unit.

**Sentinel-2 map tiles.** Offline map tiles from Sentinel-2 Level-2A provide true-colour imagery at 10 m resolution from two acquisition dates. The tiles are accessed anonymously through Microsoft Planetary Computer, with scene IDs, processing baselines, product metadata, request patterns and fetch receipts preserved in the data assets.

**NASADEM terrain.** NASADEM Merged DEM Global 1 arc second provides surface elevation data. NASADEM is a surface model that includes trees and houses. Height grids for field 3D terrain models are preserved with acquisition notes and bounds.

**NASA SMAP L4 soil moisture.** NASA SMAP L4 Global provides volumetric soil moisture (m³/m³) at ~9 km resolution as a model-assimilated product combining SMAP radiometer observations with land surface model simulations. Values include SFMC (0–5 cm), RZMC (root zone 0–100 cm), and percentiles. This is distinct from the POWER 0–1 wetness index.

**History.** The 2021–2025 archive aggregates daily values to months. Rain and ETo are totals. Temperature and wetness are means. Valid counts are included. A detailed UTC 2024 replay supports daily QA. Four other years supply the same-month reference. This short comparison is not a long-term climate normal.

**Optical quality.** Map images have real acquisition dates of 28 January 2024 (Landsat 8) and 9 March 2024 (Sentinel-2A). Four Landsat windows per site measure vegetation and cloud losses in 2024. Each window covers 900 × 900 m on a 30 m grid in UTM 45N. QA_PIXEL bits 1–4 flag dilated cloud, cirrus, cloud and shadow. Fill is excluded from the cloud-loss denominator. NDVI additionally excludes snow, saturated red/NIR, terrain occlusion and invalid reflectance. Reflectance = DN × 0.0000275 − 0.2. NDVI = (NIR − red) / (NIR + red), averaged over usable pixels. The farmer view withholds greenness below 25% valid coverage. Researchers can inspect surviving samples and counts. The 25% threshold is a demo display choice.

**Rain QA.** POWER minus IMERG on matching dates measures inter-product disagreement. The band displays the monthly 10th–90th percentile of daily differences. The approximate interval for the annual mean uses 1.96 times the standard error under daily independence. Serial correlation can narrow it unrealistically. Gauge bias uses only independently entered, date-matched 24-hour UTC totals. Multiple readings on one date are averaged.

**Rotation fit.** Irrigation, budget, Aman harvest and disruption delay determine whether an option needs review. The late-November harvest and 14-day flood checks are screening assumptions. Feasible rotations rank first. The score is the normalized weighted sum of water, soil, cost and risk fit. All-zero priorities produce no score. Water fit uses the sum of positive monthly crop-demand gaps above 65% of IMERG rain. FAO-56 Penman–Monteith ETo uses daily POWER temperature, radiation, wind, humidity and pressure. Mean relative humidity is an approximation and daily soil heat flux is set to zero. Representative crop coefficients are 1.10 for rice, 0.85 for pulse/mustard and 0.95 for potato. Relative water fit = 1 − 0.85 × gap / maximum compared gap. All-zero gaps receive equal water fit. Soil, cost and risk ratings are design judgments. Calendars approximate field occupancy in whole months. Trial irrigation totals remain independent evidence. Preference fit has no probability or calibrated yield interpretation.

**Planting.** Positions = usable hectares × 10,000 / (row metres × within-row metres). Rice is counted in hills. Broadcast mustard uses a BARI Shorisha-14 reference of 7 kg/ha. Harvest illustration = usable area × published trial yield range. The flat model draws representative plants. Actual emergence, yield and microtopography are unknown.

**Fertilizer.** Product kg = net elemental target kg/ha × treated hectares / product fraction. BARC uses elemental N/P/K. Urea has 0.46 N, TSP 0.20 P and MOP 0.50 K. A lab or officer supplies the target, after crediting prior inputs. Mixed products require joint accounting.

**Aquifer scenario.** ETc = Kc × ETo. Annual net irrigation sums positive monthly ETc minus effective rain, plus extra losses entered by the user. Pumped m³ = net mm × field hectares × 10 / efficiency × groundwater share. Allocated recharge m³ = recharge mm/year × accounting hectares × 10 × allocation share. The scenario subtracts farm and other allocated withdrawals. Multi-year arithmetic repeats the same annual conditions. Recharge and accounting area start blank. Water-table depth and drawdown need aquifer measurements. AEZ-3 classes supply regional context.

**Recovery and research.** Tree options carry evidence geography, site fit and timing conditions. Fire recovery begins with assessment of surviving vegetation. Records and photos stay in this browser. Review and consent are reported by the person entering the observation. A deployed research service would require authenticated review and independent outcome validation.
