**30-second version.** “In Rangpur, the decision after Aman depends on time, water and what a family can afford. Ropon Radar combines five years of NASA regional conditions with published local crop trials. Farmers compare complete rotations and see how many planting positions their field could hold. Extension officers can add measured yields, soil tests and gauge readings. When sensors disagree, we show the difference. When clouds hide the field, we show the gap. The farmer keeps the final decision.”

## Disaster recovery extension (45 seconds)

Open Disaster recovery. Choose Drought and Try a worked example. A February assessment fits mungbean, with the sowing window shortened to leave the next crop free by 5 May. Show the real calendar sources and the separate historical NASA panel.

Switch to Fire and load its example. The possible-contamination check holds food-crop planting. Explain that only a real qualified clearance should change that input outside the worked example.

Switch to Flood and load its example. A September Aman possibility requires already-raised photoperiod-sensitive seedlings. Open its timing evidence and show the conditional wetland-border tree guidance. Save and preview the plan.
