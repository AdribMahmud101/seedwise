## Recording tips
- Use a fresh browser profile (so first-load toasts play) at 1920×1080; record a phone segment at 412 px wide for the chat or clinic.
- Save the plan once before recording if you want the bell to already show a Planty suggestion.
- The pipeline and toasts take ~10–20 s — speed them up 1.5× in editing rather than skipping.
- Avoid showing the hidden `#research` and `#water` routes.
